## Core editorial principle

Humans stop scrolling after a visual hook, then keep watching because of repetition, process rhythm, and curiosity.

Visual hook means:
- A strong visual change that catches the eye within 0.5 seconds.
- A strange, unfamiliar, or satisfying transformation before the viewer fully understands what is being made.
- A material being pressed, cut, extruded, poured, stretched, rotated, shaped, stacked, or transformed.
- A repeated mechanical action that creates rhythm.
- A close-up of hands, tools, machine parts, or material interaction.

## A-grade manufacturing scene criteria

When scanning a process video, detect A-grade scenes with these cues:
- Human-machine synchronization: the worker's hand/body timing matches a machine cycle.
- Instant transformation: the material changes shape, texture, color, or function within 1-2 seconds.
- Rhythmic audio or repeated cycle: press/cut/pull/pour/rotate/reset repeats with a clear beat.

Cut point rules:
- In-point: 0.5 seconds before hand/tool/material enters the work zone or touches the machine.
- Peak: press, cut, bend, pour, impact, extrusion, spark, or visible transformation.
- Out-point: the processed part exits the work zone or the worker/machine resets for the next cycle.

Every meaningful scene should be scored with:
- tempo_score: 1-5
- tension_score: 1-5
- transformation_score: 1-5
- framing_score: 1-5
- flow_score: 1-5
- a_grade_score: total 1-25
- scene_role: ambient, raw_material, action, result, inspection, reset, transition

High-value highlight scenes:
- Shape transformation: material suddenly changes form.
- Mechanical repetition: press/cut/pull/extrude/rotate cycles.
- Curiosity gap: the viewer wonders "What is this?" or "How does this become the final object?"
- Strong color/texture contrast.
- Close-up where the material/action dominates the frame.
- Worker hands are acceptable, but the focus must be the process/material, not the person.

Low-value highlight scenes:
- Final product only.
- Packaging, sorting, carrying, or stacking without strong transformation.
- Static overview shots.
- Human face/body without clear material interaction.
- Explanation-only scenes.

Highlight selection should prefer scenes with:
- high visual_hook_score

## Caption manuscript quality rules

Full Draft captions are not keyword labels. They are a short on-screen manuscript.
- Each caption line must be a complete natural phrase that can stand alone on screen.
- Do not split grammar across lines. Never output a line that ends with only a particle or unfinished verb stem.
- Bad Korean fragments: "금속 망치 만드는", "먼저 손잡이를", "단단하게 고정", "작업 완료".
- Good Korean captions: "금속 망치를", "만드는 과정", "손잡이를 끼우고", "단단히 맞춰요".
- The Full Draft should feel like a compact explanation flow: curiosity hook -> what is being made -> material/process -> satisfying action -> result/finish.
- `full_caption_script_ko` is the authoritative Full Draft manuscript. `full_metadata_ko.onscreen_subtitles` must match that same quality and must not be a weaker keyword summary.
- `highlight_metadata.onscreen_caption_block` is the authoritative Japanese Highlight screen block.
- English fallback is forbidden in Korean Full and Japanese Highlight production fields.

### Korean Full narration rewrite rules

For `full_caption_script_ko`, scene data is reference material only. Do not copy `visual_summary`, `caption_text_ko`, or `screen_captions_ko` as the script.
- First imagine one hidden Korean narration that a friendly process-channel host would say to the viewer, then split it into short caption items.
- Use the visible scenes to ground facts, timing, tools, materials, and process order, but rewrite them into fresh spoken Korean narration.
- The script must sound connected when read top to bottom, not like separate scene descriptions.
- The assigned hook type is chosen by code. Follow the assigned hook type exactly when it is provided.
- Do not write the literal sentence "이게 뭔지 아세요?" anywhere. Use a fresh opening every time.
- If the opening hides the identity, reveal the object/product/material name later in the manuscript. If you ask a question, answer it. Hooks without payoff are forbidden.
- Do not fill process lines with decorative adjectives such as 정성껏, 섬세한, 거침없이, 반짝이는. Prefer a reason, a risk, or a concrete number.
- Prefer natural endings such as `-요`, `-죠`, `-예요`, `-해요`, `-돼요`, and short connected fragments that clearly continue the narration.
- Forbidden Korean Full endings: do not end caption items with report-style noun endings such as `~함` or `~됨`.
- Forbidden Korean Full rhythm: do not write three consecutive items ending in `합니다`. If that happens, rewrite the manuscript once before any repair fallback.
- Bad KO scene-copy rhythm: ["작업자가 볼트를 조임", "플라이어로 부품을 고정함", "기계가 금속을 누름"].
- Good KO narration rhythm: ["처음엔 작아 보여도", "작은 볼트를", "먼저 자리에 맞추고", "흔들리면 안 되니까", "플라이어로 잡아줘요", "여기서 중요한 건", "힘보다 방향이에요"].
